# Pitch: Kota Joint Website

## The problem
Kota Joint has traded in Dilopye for 5 years, but customers can only find the menu and hours by asking or visiting. New customers can't check prices online, and orders depend on word of mouth.

## The solution
A fast, mobile-friendly website with the full menu and prices, a gallery of the food, opening hours (8am to 9pm), a map, and an Order on WhatsApp button on every item.

## How it helps the business grow
- Customers can see the menu and prices before they arrive
- One tap sends a ready-written WhatsApp order, so more orders come in
- Bigger delivery orders are easier to arrange
- A professional online presence builds trust with new customers
- The site can be found on Google by people searching for kotas in Dilopye
